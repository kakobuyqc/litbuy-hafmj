The quick answer

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping finds associated with Litbuy. Useful entries include a source link, selected variant, measurements, listed price, reference images and the date checked. Treat each entry as a starting point for verification before ordering.

03 / A repeatable research routine

Turn browsing
into a useful shortlist.

Use this Litbuy spreadsheet guide to organize your research before committing to a product. A consistent set of notes makes similar-looking items easier to compare.

Find your starting point on CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose a category and a purpose.

    Define what you need: everyday footwear, an extra layer, coordinated sportswear or a finishing accessory.

  2. Capture the exact product details.

    Save the original link, color, size, material information and date checked. Keep different variants in separate notes.

  3. Compare measurements and evidence.

    Use the seller’s measurements and available close-up images. Separate visible details from assumptions about quality.

  4. Review the full purchase conditions.

    Confirm the current item price, shipping options, return terms and any applicable import costs before ordering.

04 / Compare with confidence

The details worth recording.

A practical framework for Litbuy finds and any other fashion research list.

Suggested columns for your personal product shortlist
FieldWhat to recordWhy it helps
Source & variant Original URL, seller, color, size and date checked. Helps you return to the exact item and identify changed information.
Fit & dimensions Relevant garment measurements, foot-length guidance or accessory dimensions. Supports comparisons with an item you already own.
Materials & construction Stated composition, lining, closures, stitching and visible finishing details. Keeps the comparison focused on specific product attributes.
Images & inspection Listing photos, available detail images and unresolved questions. Separates observable features from promotional claims.
Cost & conditions Listed currency, item price, shipping estimate and seller terms. Helps you assess the purchase beyond the displayed item price.

06 / Before you browse

Clear answers.
Useful next steps.

Understand what this guide offers and what to verify on the original product page.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping finds associated with Litbuy. Useful entries include the source link, selected variant, measurements, listed price, reference images and the date checked.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a browsing guide, category links and a comparison checklist. It does not host a downloadable spreadsheet or a live inventory feed.

Where do the category links lead?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing buttons open the CAFANS homepage.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the product’s actual measurements with an item you already own. For clothing, check chest, length, shoulder and waist measurements as applicable. For shoes, check the seller’s size chart and foot-length guidance.

Does a spreadsheet listing prove authenticity or quality?

No. A spreadsheet entry, brand name or illustration does not verify authenticity, seller authorization or product quality. Review the original listing and supporting information before making a purchase.

What should I check before ordering internationally?

Check the selected variant, measurements, seller terms, shipping availability, estimated shipping charges and any applicable import costs. Confirm current details with the service handling your order.
